Open cluster distances from Gaia parallaxes

Query Gaia DR3 stars around a cluster, pick out the stars that move together across the sky, and average their parallaxes to get the cluster's distance.

Try:

  1. Query the field around a cluster.
  2. Lasso the tight clump of co-moving stars in the proper-motion plot (and optionally a region of the sky).
  3. Check the colour–magnitude diagram: members form a clean main sequence.
  4. Read the mean parallax and the distance below.

Parallax zero-point −0.017 mas from Lindegren et al.
